Leaching

This refers to the process by which essential nutrients, such as water-soluble vitamins are washed out during cooking, processing, or preparation.

Leftovers

This refers to the remaining portion of a meal or food item that have not been consumed during the initial serving. Leftovers are intended to be consumed at a later time.

Mint leaves

These are small, aromatic, and green leaves widely used as a culinary herb due to their refreshing flavour and pleasant aroma.

Mocktail

This is a combination of various juices, syrups, and other non-alcoholic beverages, mixed to create a flavourful and refreshing drink.

Non-communicable disease (NCD)

This is a disease that is not transmissible from one person to another.

Nutrients

These are chemical substances found in food that are essential for the growth, development, and maintenance of the human body.

Osteomalacia

This is a condition characterized by the softening and weakening of bones, primarily due to the deficiency of vitamin D in adults.

Perishable foods

These are foods that have a limited shelf life and can spoil or deteriorate quickly if not stored properly.

Personal hygiene

Refers to the practice of maintaining cleanliness and taking care of one’s body to promote good health and prevent the spread of infections and diseases.

Punch

A beverage made by a combination of fruit juices and carbonated drink served at social gatherings, parties, or other events.

Roughage

This is an indigestible plant material present in food roughage cannot be digested by human enzymes.
