Improves growth and development: Proper nutrition is crucial during growth and development stages, especially in childhood and adolescence. Essential nutrients support proper physical growth. Undernutrition during childhood can lead to stunted growth.

Improves bone health: A healthy meal rich in Calcium and vitamin D is crucial for maintaining good bone health and preventing conditions such as osteomalacia, which is characterized by softening and weakening of bones due to the deficiency of vitamin D, Calcium or phosphorus. Adequate intake of these nutrients, along with other bone-supporting nutrients, including vitamin K, magnesium and protein is essential for strong and healthy bones throughout life.

Relationship between diet, physical activity and health

A diet refers to the food that a person normally eats. It is sometimes changed to control the intake of certain foods to prevent, treat or manage certain health conditions. This kind of diet is known as a special diet. Examples of special diets include low-fat, low-carbohydrate, low salt and low-sugar diets.

Diet, physical activity, and health are interconnected. When they are balanced, they have a positive impact on the overall well-being of a person. Physical activity is described as any movement of the body caused by skeletal muscles; such a movement requires the use of energy. Performing regular physical activities reduces the risk of becoming overweight and obese which are risky factors for the development of non-communicable diseases (NCDs), also known as chronic diseases, such as coronary heart diseases, type 2 diabetes and some cancers. Examples of physical activities are walking, running, dancing, swimming, and gardening.

The following aspects show the relationship between diet, physical activity and health:

(i)

A balanced meal and regular physical activities are vital for a healthy bodyweight. A nutrient-rich meal along with regular physical activities can prevent excessive weight gain, thus maintaining ideal body weight.

(ii)

A balanced meal and physical activity benefit heart health. A low-fat and low-sodium meals and regular physical activity can lower blood pressure, reduce cholesterol, and lower the risk of heart disease.

(iii)

Proper nutrition, especially an adequate intake of calcium and vitamin D, is essential for maintaining good teeth and bone health. When combined with physical activities, it further supports bone strength and overall well-being.
