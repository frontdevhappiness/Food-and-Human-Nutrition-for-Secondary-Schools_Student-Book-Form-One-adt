Cowpea cutlets

Ingredients (serve 4 people)

250 g cowpeas 4 cloves of garlic
125 g potatoes 1 red sweet pepper
2 eggs 2 g ground pepper (optional)
4 g salt 30 g breadcrumbs
3 lettuce leaves 30 g parsley
500 ml cooking oil 1 onion
1 carrot Water
Coriander leaves

Method

1.

Chop the onion, carrot and garlic. Then, cut the coriander leaves.

2.

Peel the potatoes; wash and boil them with the carrot, garlic and coriander leaves.

3.

Cook the cowpeas and chopped onion gently until tender and only a little water is left.

4.

Mix the potato with cowpeas and mash them well to get a smooth paste. Add parsley, salt and seasonings.

5.

Beat the eggs. Add sufficient beaten eggs to the mixture to bind it into a stiff paste. If it is too moist, add some breadcrumbs.

6.

Shape the paste into rounds or squares. Coat well with the remaining eggs and breadcrumbs. Then, deep-fry them until crispy and brown.

7.

Serve them on a bed of lettuce and garnish with a diced red sweet pepper or any suitable garnish, as shown in Figure 6.6.
