Frying: Frying is a method of cooking food in hot fat or oil. This quick, convenient and popular method of cooking involves using a high temperature. This method is divided into dry frying, shallow frying and deep frying. Vegetables use shallow and deep frying methods.

(i)

Shallow frying involves cooking food using a small amount of oil in a shallow pan. For example, eggplant and cauliflower can be fried using this method.

(ii)

Deep frying involves cooking by completely immersing the food in hot oil. Examples of deep-fried foods include fried vegetable samosas, and potato chips.

Observe the following rules when frying food

(i)Heat the oil to the right temperature.
(ii)Put the food item into the oil carefully to avoid spilling.
(iii)Use fresh and clean fat and oil.
(iv)Thoroughly drain foods that are to be fried, such as potato chips, before frying them.
(v)For deep frying, the oil should be two-thirds of the pan to avoid overflowing.
(vi)You can coat deep-fried foods such as kachori to prevent breakage.
(vii)Do not overfill the food in a pan to provide enough spacing for proper cooking.
