Caffeine can have a disruptive effect on sleep when taken in excess while increasing energy production, hence making the body alert and awake. Excessive consumption of caffeine may cause restlessness and nausea in addition to increasing heart rate. Traditional tea, commonly known as black tea, contains polyphenols, which are natural compounds that can protect the body against cancer. Comparatively, the quantity of polyphenols is higher in green tea than in black tea. This makes green tea more beneficial than black tea.

Recipes for preparing tea, coffee and cocoa

Black tea

Ingredients (Serve 3 people)

1 teaspoon of tea leaves or 1 teabag

2 g tea spices

1 tablespoon sugar or honey (optional)

1 lemon (optional)

500 ml of water

Method

  1. 1. Warm up the teapot before making tea in it.
  2. 2. Put tea leaves and spices in the teapot.
  3. 3. Boil water and pour it into the teapot.
  4. 4. Cover the teapot and allow the tea leaves to infuse for 3-5 minutes. A prolonged infusion may make the tea bitter.
  5. 5. Strain your tea into another warmed teapot.
  6. 6. Pour it into a tea cup and add milk, sugar, honey or lemon (Optional).
  7. 7. Serve it as shown in Figure 5.7.
