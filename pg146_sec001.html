Method

1.
Mix all the types of flour. Add baking powder and then sieve.
2.
Mix the flour with salt and ground garlic.
3.
Chop the onion, green pepper and carrot. Then, sauté and add to the flour.
4.
Add the cut coriander leaves, beaten egg and milk or water. Then, stir the mixture to make a thick paste.
5.
Heat the oil in a deep-frying pan.
6.
Drop a tablespoonful of paste at a time in the heated oil. Then, fry it in moderate heat until golden brown. Then, serve them as shown in Figure 6.7.
