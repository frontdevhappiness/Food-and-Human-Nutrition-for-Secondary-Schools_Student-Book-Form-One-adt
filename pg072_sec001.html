Methods of cooking vegetables

Common methods of cooking vegetables include boiling, braising, stewing, frying and steaming. Choose a method that will retain as many nutrients as possible.

Boiling: This is the simplest and the most common method of cooking food, including vegetables. It involves submerging food in the boiling water or other liquids and cooking it until it becomes tender and fully cooked. Boiling is used for a wide range of foods including vegetables such as green peas, carrots, broccoli, eggplants and French beans. When boiling, cover the pot to prevent water vapour from escaping, thus retaining nutrients and enabling the temperature to rise more quickly so that the food boils fast. Covering the pot helps in retaining nutrients. Observe the following rules during boiling:

(i) Use moderate water to boil vegetables such as carrots, broccoli and eggplants to retain their flavour. Too much water on vegetables leads to a less flavourful end product.
