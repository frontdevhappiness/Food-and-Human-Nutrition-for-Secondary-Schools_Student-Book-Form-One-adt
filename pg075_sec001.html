Stewing: Stewing is a moist-heat cooking method that involves a slow and gentle cooking of food in a covered pot with a small amount of liquid. This method is particularly suitable for cooking vegetables such as green peas, mushrooms, celery, leeks, mustard greens, eggplants, tomatoes, kale and peppers. It makes the food tender and infused with delicious tastes of the cooking liquid.

General rules for stewing food

To achieve delicious and well-cooked stews, follow these general rules:

  • (i)

    Use a heavy-bottomed pot with a tight-fitting lid to ensure an even heat distribution and moisture retention during cooking.

  • (ii)

    Cut the food into uniform pieces to ensure even cooking.

  • (iii)

    Once the ingredients and liquid are mixed, bring the mixture to a boil; reduce the heat to a gentle simmer. Cooking the stew slowly over low heat softens the ingredients and allows the flavour to blend together.

  • (iv)

    Add sufficient amount of liquid or stock to obtain enough stew.

  • (v)

    Serve the food with the stew coming from cooking.

  • (vi)

    Always cook the stew in the covered pot to retain steam and maintain a consistent temperature. This helps to tenderise the ingredients and prevents excessive evaporation.

  • (vii)

    Stir the stew occasionally to ensure even cooking and prevent sticking at the bottom.

  • (viii)

    If your stew includes both meat and vegetables, add the vegetables later in the cooking process to avoid overcooking them. You may add dense vegetables such as carrots and potatoes at the beginning and more delicate vegetables such as peas and leaf vegetables towards the end.

Advantages of stewing food

  • (i)

    Stewing is an economical method of cooking since it uses little fuel.

  • (ii)

    Stews do not require much attention during cooking as they need only a small amount of heat during cooking.

  • (iii)

    Nutrients are not lost because the stock is retained as part of the stew. While some water-soluble nutrients may leach into the cooking liquid, stewing preserves significant amount of vitamins and minerals in the ingredients. This makes the stew a nutritious and wholesome meal.
