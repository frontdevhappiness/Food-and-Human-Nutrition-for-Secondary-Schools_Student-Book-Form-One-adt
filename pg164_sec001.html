Psychotherapy

Psychotherapy, also known as talk therapy, is a crucial component of eating disorder treatment that is done by mental health professionals. It involves regular personal interaction to help an individual change bad behaviour. Family-based therapy is among the treatments commonly used to treat eating disorders. These therapies can help individuals develop healthier thoughts and emotions as well as better behaviours on food, body image and self-esteem.

Nutrition counselling

Working with a nutritionist or registered dietitian who specializes in eating disorders can be beneficial in creating a balanced and healthy meal plan. Nutrition counselling helps individuals develop a healthier relationship with food and establish regular eating patterns and improved behaviours.

Addressing other existing disorders

It is very common for eating disorders to occur with other mental health conditions, such as depression and anxiety. Identifying and treating any existing disorders is crucial for comprehensive recovery.

Avoiding triggers

Identifying and minimizing triggers that increase disordered eating behaviours is important. This includes limiting exposure to social media or content that promotes unrealistic body ideas or unhealthy eating behaviours.
