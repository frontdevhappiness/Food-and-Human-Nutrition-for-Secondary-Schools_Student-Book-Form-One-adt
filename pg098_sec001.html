Braised peas and carrots

Ingredients (serve 6 people)

  • 250 g green peas
  • 6 medium-size carrots
  • 1 onion
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 100 g vegetable stock
  • 1 green pepper
  • 50 ml cooking oil

Method

  1. 1. Wash the peas, dice the carrots and chop the onion and green pepper.
  2. 2. Heat the cooking oil in a pan and fry the onion lightly.
  3. 3. Add carrots, green pepper and peas and then continue frying.
  4. 4. Add water, vegetable stock and salt. Then, simmer gently until tender.
  5. 5. Serve it hot. Figure 4.30 shows braised peas and carrots.
