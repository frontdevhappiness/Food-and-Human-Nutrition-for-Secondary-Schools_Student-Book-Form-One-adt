Concept of food and nutrition

Food is anything edible that can be solid, semi-solid or liquid which when eaten, digested and assimilated in the body provides essential substances called nutrients. Nutrients keep the person alive, provide energy, enable growth, repair worn-out tissues and protect the body from diseases. Food is obtained from plants and animal sources. It can be consumed in a raw state or in a processed form.

Nutrition is the process of taking in food and converting it into energy and other vital nutrients required for life. It is concerned with how the body uses nutrients to nourish itself and perform different functions. It is the study of all processes of growth, maintenance and repair of worn-out tissues in the body to remain healthy.
