Appetizers

An appetizer is a solid or liquid food taken before a main meal to stimulate the appetite. An appetizer is also known as a starter and is usually served in small portions. Appetizers are often savoury and flavourful, designed to stimulate the taste buds and leave eaters excited for the rest of the meal. Appetizers can take various forms, and their preparation can vary depending on cultural and regional culinary traditions.

Types of appetizers

There are several types of appetizers. The commonest ones include cocktails, mocktails, Hors d’oeuvres and soups.

Cocktails

A cocktail is a drink prepared by using different drinks, one of them being an alcoholic drink. Cocktails are usually served as a first-course meal. They can be prepared by blending, shaking and stirring methods. An example of a cocktail drink is a fruit punch.

Mocktail

A mocktail is a non-alcoholic beverage resembling a cocktail in appearance, presentation, and flavour. It includes the addition of different fruits, syrup, juices, or cream. Mocktails are designed to provide the same enjoyable and refreshing experience as cocktails, but without alcohol. Mocktails are an excellent alternative for individuals who choose not to consume alcohol, including pregnant women, designated drivers, and those who prefer non-alcoholic options for various reasons. They are also suitable for social gatherings where guests of all ages can participate in the celebration without alcohol. Like cocktails, mocktails can come in various flavours and styles, using a wide range of ingredients such as fruit juices, syrups, carbonated drinks, fresh fruits, herbs and spices. Mocktails are often garnished with fruits, herbs, and colourful straws making them visually appealing and enjoyable. Examples of mocktails include an orange, pineapple and lemonade drink as well as a mint and ginger lemon drink.
