Frying: This is a quick, convenient and popular method of cooking, which involves using high temperature. Frying involves deep frying, shallow frying and dry frying. Cooking nuts uses the dry-frying method, which does not involve adding any oil or fat to the food.

Roasting: This is a dry method of cooking. Nuts can be roasted in an oven or open fire. When roasting nuts, stir them frequently to avoid burning. It is better to preheat the oven before roasting the nuts as this will ensure even cooking. When roasting nuts in an oven or open fire, you should not cover the pan.
