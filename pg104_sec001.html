Apple punch

Ingredients (serve 4 people)

  • 3 fresh medium apples
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 2 passion fruits
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 glasses of water
  • 1 glass carbonated drink
  • Fresh mint leaves (optional)
  • Ice cubes

Method

  1. 1. Wash the apples and cut them to remove the core.
  2. 2. Cut the apples into small pieces, sprinkle lemon juice on them, and then prepare apple juice.
  3. 3. Wash and cut passion fruits. Then, scoop the inside sac containing juice and small seeds.
  4. 4. Prepare passion juice.
  5. 5. Mix the juices, add sugar, stir well and then strain it into a jug.
  6. 6. Add carbonated drink.
  7. 7. Chill or add ice-cubes (Optional).
  8. 8. Add mint leaves and serve accordingly.
