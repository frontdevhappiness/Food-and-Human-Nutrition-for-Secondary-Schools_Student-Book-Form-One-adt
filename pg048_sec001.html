Taking safety precautions in the kitchen

Collect ripe mangoes from the garden or market, then follow the following procedure;

  • (i) Wash and chop the mangoes on the board using a sharp knife then serve them.
  • (ii) Wash the knife and the chopping board.
  • (iii) Put the knife in its sheath or guard.
