Bean croquettes

Ingredients (serve 4 people)

  • 250 g beans
  • 3 leaves of lettuce
  • 2 g salt
  • Water
  • 500 ml cooking oil
  • 1 onion
  • 30 g breadcrumbs
  • 1 bunch of coriander leaves
  • 1 carrot
  • 4 cloves of garlic
  • 2 eggs

Method

  1. 1.

    Sort, wash and boil the beans until soft, and only a little water is left.

  2. 2.

    Chop the onion and coriander leaves.

  3. 3.

    Drain the water completely and mash the beans into a smooth paste.

  4. 4.

    Add beaten egg yolks, curry powder, salt, onion and coriander leaves.

  5. 5.

    Mix to obtain stiff consistency and shape the mixture into croquettes of bean shape.

  6. 6.

    Deep them into the egg white. Then, roll them into breadcrumbs.

  7. 7.

    Fry them in hot oil until brown.

  8. 8.

    Serve them on a bed of lettuce and garnish with sliced boiled carrots or any suitable garnish, as shown in Figure 6.8.
