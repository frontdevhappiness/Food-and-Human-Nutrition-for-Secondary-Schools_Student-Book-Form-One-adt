(i) anaemic
(ii) overweight
(iii) vegetarians

Points to consider when selecting vegetables

Selecting vegetables requires careful attention to ensure their quality. This depends on the type of vegetables needed. The following tips are worth considering:

Green leafy vegetables

  • (i) Select fresh green-coloured vegetables. Handle the vegetables with care to avoid damage because, once damaged, they attract spoilage microorganisms.
  • (ii) Green leafy vegetables should be firm and crispy.
  • (iii) Avoid selecting wilted and bruised leafy vegetables as they have lost some nutrients, such as vitamins A and C.
  • (iv) The stem should be medium-sized and break easily.
  • (v) Vegetable leaves should be tender, and their ribs should break easily.

Root vegetables

  • (i) Select firm root vegetables.
  • (ii) The skin should be smooth, with no wrinkles, and damage to prevent them from microbial spoilage.
  • (iii) Root vegetables should be free from spade marks, which make them vulnerable to spoilage.
  • (iv) Root vegetables should have no signs of sprouting.
  • (v) Select evenly-sized root vegetables for easy preparation and cooking.
  • (vi) Root vegetables, such as carrots, should be fresh with green tops.
  • (vii) To avoid food poisoning, avoid insect- or mould-infected root vegetables.
  • (viii) Avoid discoloured or decayed root vegetables.
