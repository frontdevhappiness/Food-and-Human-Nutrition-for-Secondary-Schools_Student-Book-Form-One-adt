Spinach stuffed mushroom

Ingredients (serve 4 person)
4 large mushrooms
2 eggs
5g grounded garlic
1 bunch spinach
200g cheese
200g breadcrumbs
Grounded pepper (optional)
Pinch of salt

Method

  1. 1. Preheat the oven to 150°C.
  2. 2. Put the mushrooms on the baking sheet in an arranged order.
  3. 3. Bake the mushroom until tender for about 12 minutes.
  4. 4. Drain any juice accumulated from the mushroom.
  5. 5. Beat eggs. Add garlic, salt and pepper and then mix them well.
  6. 6. Chop spinach thinly.
  7. 7. Mix spinach, 100g cheese and 100g breadcrumbs into the egg mixture then stir them well.
  8. 8. Divide the spinach mixture over the mushroom caps.
  9. 9. Sprinkle the mushroom with the remaining cheese and breadcrumbs.
  10. 10. Return the mushroom to the oven and bake for about 10 minutes until the topping is golden brown, and the cheese is melted.
  11. 11. Serve it accordingly.
