Micronutrients

They are required by the body in small amounts. Micronutrients include vitamins and minerals. Although micro-nutrients are needed in small amounts, they are essential for the body to function well.

Vitamins: These are chemical compounds that the body needs in small amounts to help it function properly. Although they are needed in small amounts, they are essential for various bodily processes and overall health. Inadequate supply of vitamins in the diet may result in deficiency disorders.

Vitamins are classified into two groups, as follows:

(i) Fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, and K)

These vitamins are soluble in fats and are stored in the body’s cells. An adequate amount of these nutrients should be taken. Consumption of excess amounts of fat-soluble vitamins may become toxic to the body.

(ii) Water-soluble vitamins (B and C)

These vitamins are soluble in water; the body needs them daily in recommended amounts. Water-soluble vitamins are not stored in the body. The excess amounts of water-soluble vitamins are excreted through urine. Hence, it is not likely to reach toxic levels.
