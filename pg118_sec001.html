Preparation of a mocktail

The preparation of a mocktail is straightforward and enjoyable. The preparation of a mocktail requires non-alcoholic drinks such as fruit juices and carbonated drinks. A sweetener (optional), such as syrup, honey, or fruit syrups, may be added to enhance the flavour. Flavourings such as herbs, spices, or syrups are added to create unique and refreshing flavours. Ice cubes or refrigerators are essential for chilling mocktails.

Recipes for preparing mocktails

Recipes for preparing orange pineapple and lemonade drink and mint and ginger lemon drink.

Orange pineapple and lemonade drink

Ingredients (serve 3 people)

  • 4 oranges
  • 1 pineapple
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 teaspoon sugar
  • 2 ice cubes
  • Mint leaf
  • 300 ml carbonated drink

Method

  • 1. Wash all the fruits.
  • 2. Peel and cut pineapples into small pieces.
  • 3. Prepare pineapple juice.
  • 4. Squeeze oranges to obtain juice.
  • 5. Squeeze lemon to obtain juice.
  • 6.
    Mix all the juice, carbonated drink and sugar. Then stir the mixture well.
  • 7. Add ice cubes and mint leaves.
  • 8. Serve accordingly.
