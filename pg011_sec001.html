Protective foods: Foods rich in minerals and vitamins are known as protective foods. They protect the body against diseases. Examples of protective foods include fruits and vegetables.

Classification of food based on its chemical composition

The chemical composition of food is important since it determines its utilization in the body. This classification is based on the types and amount of nutrients present in the food. Carbohydrates, proteins and lipids form the major chemical components of foods since they are available in large quantities. Vitamins and minerals form the minor chemical components of food since they are present in smaller quantities in foods.

Classification of food based on the chemical properties of nutrients

Under this type of classification, food is classified based on the chemical properties of nutrients such as organic and inorganic nutrients. Nutrients such as carbohydrates, protein, fat and vitamins contain elements of carbon. These are called organic nutrients; and nutrients that do not contain carbon elements such as minerals are called inorganic nutrients.

Classification of food based on its origin

Depending on its origin, food can be classified based on the sources. Foods which are obtained from animals are called animal food sources, whereas foods from plants are called plant food sources.
