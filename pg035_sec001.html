The kitchen

Introduction

The kitchen is one of the important places found at home. It should be well equipped to facilitate efficient preparation and cooking of food. In this chapter, you will learn about the kitchen, hygiene in food preparation and cooking and safety in the kitchen. The competencies developed will enable you to take proper care of the kitchen to avoid accidents and to prepare, cook, and serve food hygienically.

Concept of a kitchen

A kitchen is a room or an area inside or outside the house or commercial establishment where food is prepared, cooked, and sometimes served. Other related activities such as washing utensils are also performed in the kitchen. It should be well-planned to increase efficiency and to save time and energy. When the kitchen is well planned, the utensils are in appropriate order to form a continuous working area which suits the sequence and stages of the main activities to be carried out in the kitchen. It is important to arrange fixed equipment in sequence to suit the activities carried out in the kitchen to save time for work and avoid unnecessary movements. It can be built either inside or outside the main house.

Types of kitchens

Two main types of kitchens are commonly found in residential settings. These are open kitchens and separate kitchens.
