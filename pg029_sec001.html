The retention of nutrients during food storage can be done in the following ways:

  • (i) Fruits and vegetables should be stored for a short time to maintain their freshness, nutrients, flavour and overall quality.
  • (ii) Keep milk in covered and coloured containers to prevent loss of vitamin B2.
  • (iii) Store food in a cool environment away from direct sunlight as this can lead to loss of nutrients such as vitamins B2 and C.
  • (iv) Store iodized salt in a cool place as heat decreases the iodine present in the salt.
