Preface

This textbook, Food and Human Nutrition for Secondary Schools has been written specifically for Form One students in the United Republic of Tanzania. The textbook has been prepared in accordance with the 2023 Food and Human Nutrition syllabus for Ordinary Secondary Education, Form I-IV, issued by the Ministry of Education Science and Technology (MoEST). Furthermore, some of the contents are adapted from three textbooks, namely Home Economics for Secondary School, Student’s Book, Form Two (2021); Food and Human Nutrition, Student’s Book, Form Three; and Food and Human Nutrition Student’s Book, Form Four (2022) which were published in accordance with the 2019 syllabus issued by the Ministry of Education Science and Technology.

The textbook consists of seven (7) chapters, namely Introduction to food and nutrition; Food groups; The kitchen; Fruits and vegetables; Beverages and appetizers; Pulses, nuts and oil seeds; and Proper nutrition. Besides, each chapter contains activities, illustrations, and exercises. You are also required to prepare a portfolio for keeping records of the entire lessons. You are encouraged to do all the activities and attempt all the questions. Doing so will enhance your understanding and development of the intended competencies for this level.

Additional learning resources are available in the TIE e-library at https://ol.tie.go.tz or ol.tie.go.tz
