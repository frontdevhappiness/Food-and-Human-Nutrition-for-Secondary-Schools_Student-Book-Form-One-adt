1 clove garlicA pinch of chilli powder (optional)2 g salt60 ml cooking oilWater
1 tomato

Method

  1. 1. Remove the stalks from the brinjals. Wash and cut them into the desired shape.
  2. 2. Wash, peel and cut the potatoes.
  3. 3. Chop the onions and green pepper. Crush the garlic.
  4. 4. Chop the tomatoes.
  5. 5. Heat the oil and fry the onions until golden brown.
  6. 6. Add brinjals and potatoes. Then, fry for 2 minutes while stirring the mixture well.
  7. 7. Add dry ingredients and garlic, and continue frying for 2 more minutes
  8. 8. Add tomato, tomato puree, green pepper and water. Then, cook gently until tender.
  9. 9. Serve it as shown in Figure 4.28.
