Benefits of vegetables

Vegetables offer the following health benefits to the human body:

(i)
Vegetables are rich sources of micronutrients such as vitamins and minerals. The vitamins found in most vegetables include vitamins A, B and C. The minerals found in vegetables include iron, magnesium and zinc.
(ii)
Vegetables contain antioxidants such as carotenoids. These antioxidants protect the body against chronic diseases such as cancer.
(iii)
Vegetables are low in fat but high in fibre; hence, consuming them reduces the risk of overweight, obesity, cardiovascular disease and diabetes.
(iv)
Fibre in vegetables helps to absorb water and expands as it passes through the digestive system. This process facilitates bowel movement and prevents constipation.

Classification of vegetables

The classification of vegetables depends on the part of the plant from which they come. These classifications include leafy, root, tuber, fruit, stem, seed, flower and fungi vegetables.
