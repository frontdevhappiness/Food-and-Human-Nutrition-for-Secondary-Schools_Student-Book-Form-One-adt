Glossary

Anxiety

This is a feeling of unease, worry, or fear about a future event or situation, which can be real or imagined

Baking powder

This is a leavening agent in powder form, commonly used in baking to help dough and batter rise.

Broth

This is a flavourful liquid made by simmering meat, fish, or vegetables in water. It is commonly used as a base for soups, stews, sauces, and other dishes.

Burns

This refers to injuries to the skin and underlying tissues caused by exposure to heat, fire, chemicals or electricity.

Blanching

A short heat treatment applied to vegetables and fruits using hot water or steam.

Caffeine

This is a natural stimulant found in various plants such as coffee, tea and cocoa.

Cocktail

This is a mixed drink typically made by combining various alcoholic beverages with other ingredients such as fruit juices, soda or syrups.

Cross-contamination

This is a process by which harmful micro-organisms transfer from raw foods such as meat, fish and vegetables to cooked foods. These micro-organisms can also transfer through the use of kitchen utensils to food during food preparation, cooking and serving.

Eating disorders

These are serious health conditions characterized by abnormal eating habits that negatively impact a person’s physical and emotional well-being.

Food preservation

This refers to the process of treating and handling food in a way that extends its shelf life and maintains its quality and safety for longer periods.

Food processing

This is any method used to turn fresh foods into food products that are suitable for consumption.

Garnish

This refers to the decorative elements added to a savoury dish to enhance appearance, flavour, and overall presentation
