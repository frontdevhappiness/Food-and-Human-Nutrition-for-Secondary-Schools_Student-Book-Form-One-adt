Coffee

Ingredients (Serve 1 person)
  • 1 tablespoon ground coffee
  • 1 tablespoon sugar or honey (optional)
  • 500 ml of water

Method

  1. 1. Warm up a coffee jug and then add coffee.
  2. 2. Pour boiling water into the jug. Never allow coffee to boil because it speeds up caffeine extraction, which may result in a bitter taste.
  3. 3. Allow the coffee to stand for 10 minutes for the aroma to infuse.
  4. 4. Strain the coffee into a warmed coffee pot.
  5. 5. Serve coffee with milk or sugar (optional).
