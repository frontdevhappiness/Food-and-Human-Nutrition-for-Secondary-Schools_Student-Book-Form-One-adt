Benefits of salads

Besides their nutritional value, salads add colour to a prepared meal. A meal with a salad is appetising, making an individual eat enough food and satisfy hunger. The following are the benefits of salads in the body.

Salads provide enough vitamins and minerals. The consumption of fruits and vegetables provides enough vitamins and minerals to maintain a healthy body and mind. Salads are also full of flavour and have a satisfying crunch. They are rich in antioxidants such as carotenoids, and vitamins C and E. Hence, salads can protect the body against various types of cancer.

Salads provide fibre. Fibre prevents constipation by softening human excrement. Fibre also lowers body cholesterol and regulates blood sugar, hence preventing the risk of heart disease and diabetes. Salads satisfy hunger as they make someone feel full for a long time. Adding a healthy salad to every meal can facilitate weight management. For example, a salad made with a large serving of spinach and kale creates a strong foundation for weight loss.

Salads provide fat. Some salads contain healthy fats. For example, a salad with olive oil and avocados contain healthy fats which lower cholesterol level, hence reducing the risk of heart disease.

Salads provide water. Salads contain sufficient amounts of water for the body to perform various functions. Watermelon and strawberries, for instance, contain 92 per cent of water; likewise, cucumbers and certain lettuces contain 96 per cent of water. Salads made with a combination of vegetables and fruits supplies water to the body. Grapefruits, peaches, oranges, pineapples, spinach, broccoli, and tomatoes are other fruits and vegetables with high water content.
