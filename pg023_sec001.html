Minerals: These are chemical elements or inorganic substances needed by the body to perform different essential metabolic functions. Some minerals are needed in large amounts in the body. These are called macro elements. These include calcium, iron, phosphorous, potassium, sulphur, chlorine, sodium, and magnesium. Other minerals are required in small amounts. They are called micro-elements. These include fluoride, iodine, manganese, zinc, copper and selenium.
