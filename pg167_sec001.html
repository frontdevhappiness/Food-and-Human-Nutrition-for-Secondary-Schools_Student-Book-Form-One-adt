Food and Human Nutrition for Secondary Schools: Student’s Book Form One

Sautéing
A cooking method that involves quickly cooking food in a small amount of hot oil or butter in a pan over high heat.
Scalds
A type of burn injury caused by contact with hot liquids or steam.
Seasoning
This refers to the process of enhancing the flavour of food by adding various herbs, spices, salt, or other condiments during cooking or before serving.
Smoothie
A beverage made by blending fruits, vegetables, yoghurt, milk, or other liquid bases.
Stock
Refers to a flavourful liquid made by simmering bones, fish, meat, vegetables, and aromatic ingredients in water
Syrup
This is a thick, sweet liquid made by dissolving sugar in water and heating it to create a concentrated solution. It is commonly used to make juices.
