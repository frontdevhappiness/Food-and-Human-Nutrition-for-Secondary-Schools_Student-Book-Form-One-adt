Nutrients present in different foods

Nutrients are chemical substances present in food that must be supplied to the body in suitable amounts. The amount of nutrients needed by the body depends on the age, sex, activity, and physiological and health condition of an individual. Nutrients include carbohydrates, proteins, fats, minerals, and vitamins. Water is not a nutrient, but it is used in the digestion and absorption process of nutrients. Likewise, dietary fibre, also known as roughage, is not a nutrient; but it is needed in the digestion process. The body needs a wide range of nutrients to remain healthy and maintain life. Most foods contain more than one nutrient. For example, milk contains protein, fats, vitamins and minerals. Nutrients are categorised into two main groups, namely macronutrients and micronutrients, based on the body’s requirements.

Macronutrients

These are required in large amounts in the body. They include carbohydrates, proteins and fats.
