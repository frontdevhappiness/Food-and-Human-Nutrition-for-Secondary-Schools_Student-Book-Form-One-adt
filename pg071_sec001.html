Fruit vegetables

(i)

Fruit vegetables such as cucumbers, tomatoes and pumpkins should be fresh, smooth and firm.

(ii)

Choose medium-sized fruit vegetables for easy preparation and cooking.

Seed vegetables

(i)

Select vegetables according to their quality and preparation duration. For example, green beans and green peas require a longer preparation time.

(ii)

Select vegetables that match well with other foods on the menu. Choose vegetables that are in season as they are fresh, less expensive and more readily available than off-season vegetables.

(iii)

Select firm and deeply-coloured peas.

(iv)

Select firm and non-wrinkled beans.

Bulb vegetables

(i)

Select firm medium-sized vegetables such as onions and garlic.

(ii)

Bulb vegetables should be non-bruised to avoid spoilage.

Flower vegetables

(i)

Vegetables such as broccoli and cauliflower heads should be heavy with firm hearts.

(ii)

Cauliflower should be closed, clean, white and free from any bruises. The presence of dark spots on them indicates over-maturity.

Fungi vegetables

(i)

Select fresh firm mushrooms with smooth caps.

(ii)

Select mushrooms with closed veils for a delicate flavour or open veils and exposed gills for a richer flavour.

(iii)

Mushrooms should not be slimy and should not have dark spots.

(iv)

Avoid shrivelled and bruised mushrooms. Soft and soggy mushrooms signal their spoilage.
