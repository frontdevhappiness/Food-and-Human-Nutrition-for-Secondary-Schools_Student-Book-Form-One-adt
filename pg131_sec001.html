Velouté: A velouté is a thick soup. It is made from meat, poultry, fish or vegetables. Its flavour is determined by the stock used. The soup is thickened with a blond, which is made by cooking equal amounts of wheat flour and butter over medium heat until light brown. Egg yolks and cream are added to this soup just before serving. Velouté is incredibly versatile and serves as the base for many delicious dishes, both as a sauce and as a soup. The specific name of the soup depends on the stock used; for example, chicken velouté soup is made with chicken.

Cold soups

Cold soups, also known as chilled soups, are a refreshing and appetizing culinary option, especially during hot weather. They constitute a special category because they are served chilled. Hot soups become cold soups after undergoing chilling with minor alterations made to their consistency and flavour. Cold soups can be thickened with starches. These soups are more popular in warm weather. Unlike traditional hot soups, which are served warm, cold soups are meant to be enjoyed cold or at room temperature.

International soups

International soups, also known as national soups, include various types of flavourful and unique soup recipes from various countries and cultures around the world. They represent the region of origin. Each soup reflects the culinary traditions, regional ingredients, and local preferences of its country of origin. They significantly reflect a particular culture and history. They are valued as a symbol of national identity.
