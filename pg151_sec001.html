Preparation of dry-fried groundnuts

Prepare, cook and serve dry-fried groundnuts by following the appropriate methods.

(i) Assess the quality of the prepared dish.

(ii) Why is it recommended to dry the groundnuts before dry-frying them?

Roasted cashew nuts

Ingredients (serve 6 people)

250 g raw cashew nuts

1 tablespoon oil

½ teaspoon salt (optional)

Methods

  • 1. Preheat the oven to 130°C.
  • 2. In a bowl, add oil and salt to the cashew nuts and then mix them well.
  • 3. Line the baking sheet with aluminium foil.
  • 4. Place cashew nuts on the baking sheet and put them on the middle part of the oven.
  • 5. Roast for 10 to 15 minutes and stir occasionally.

  • 6. Once golden brown, remove them from the oven and let them cool for at least 15 minutes. Then, serve it accordingly. Figure 6.11 shows roasted cashew nuts.
