Advantages of the steaming cooking method

  1. (i)

    Nutrient loss is reduced since the food does not get in contact with the cooking water directly.

  2. (ii)

    Steaming allows food to retain its natural flavours, colours, and textures. It does not require fats or oils, hence retaining the original taste of the ingredients.

  3. (iii)

    Steaming is a low-fat cooking method since it does not require the addition of fat or oils. This makes it an excellent choice for those who reduce their fat intake.

  4. (iv)

    Steaming is a straightforward cooking technique that requires minimal preparation and clean up. Once you have set up the steamer and put the food, it requires little attention, allowing you to focus on other meal preparations.

  5. (v)

    Steaming can be used for a wide range of ingredients, including vegetables, seafood, poultry, and even desserts like puddings. It is a versatile cooking method that can be adapted to various food types.

  6. (vi)

    Steaming provides even and gentle heat distribution, ensuring that the food cooks uniformly. There is no risk of uneven cooking or burnt spots, as long as you follow the appropriate cooking time.

  7. (vii)

    Steaming helps the food to retain its moisture, hence preventing it from drying out. This is particularly beneficial for delicate ingredients such as fish or seafood that can easily become overcooked and dry with other cooking methods.

  8. (viii)

    Since steaming typically involves using a steamer basket or rack, there are fewer pots and pans to clean compared to other cooking techniques.

  9. (ix)

    Foods cooked by steaming are light and easy to digest; therefore, it is a suitable cooking method for invalids and convalescents.

Disadvantages of the steaming cooking method

  1. (i)

    Extended cooking time: Steaming, especially for larger or thicker ingredients, can take longer than is the case with other cooking methods, such as boiling or frying. While steaming is gentle and preserves nutrients, it may not be the best option when you need to cook something quickly.

  2. (ii)

    A steamer can cause scalds if carelessly handled.
