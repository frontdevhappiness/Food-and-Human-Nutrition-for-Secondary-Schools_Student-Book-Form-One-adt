Peas and groundnut sauce

Ingredients (serve 4 people)

  • 120 g green peas
  • 1 medium carrot
  • 1 onion
  • 2 g salt
  • 2 g curry powder
  • 150 g grounded groundnuts
  • 15 ml cooking oil
  • 1 large tomato
  • Water
  • 3 cloves of garlic

Method

  1. 1. Wash and boil peas until soft. Separate peas from stock.
  2. 2. Slice the tomato, chop the onion and carrot and crush the garlic.
  3. 3. Heat the oil in the cooking pan and fry the onion until golden brown.
  4. 4. Add tomato, garlic, carrot, curry powder and salt and then fry for a while.
  5. 5. Mix ground groundnuts with some stocks from the peas.
  6. 6. Add the boiled peas to the fried mixture and then stir them well.
