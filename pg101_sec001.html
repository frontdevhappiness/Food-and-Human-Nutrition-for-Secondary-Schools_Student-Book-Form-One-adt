Types of Beverages

Beverages are classified into three main groups, namely refreshing or thirsty-quenching drinks, stimulating drinks and nourishing drinks.

Refreshing or thirst-quenching drinks

Refreshing drinks are beverages that are used when a person is feeling thirsty or seeking relief from heat or dehydration. These drinks are typically cold or chilled and are known for their ability to quench thirst by providing a pleasant sensation of refreshment. Some popular examples of refreshing and thirst-quenching drinks include punch and all types of juice from fruits such as passion, pineapples, and mangoes or vegetables such as carrots. Some beverages not only refresh but also provide the body with nutrients. Hence, their consumption is strongly encouraged. Most fruit and vegetable juices contain high levels of vitamins A, E and C and minerals such as potassium and sodium. Refreshing drinks also hydrate the body; thus, they alleviate dehydration-related symptoms such as thirst, mouth dryness and mental fatigue.
