Braising: This method involves a combination of the dry and moist heat cooking of vegetables. Initially, one cooks vegetables in a small amount of oil before further simmering them in a small amount of water. This method cooks vegetables slowly. The vegetables cooked using this method are well-flavoured. This method can be applied to celery, cabbage and onions.

Advantages of braising

Braising creates more tasty dishes as the food tends to have a richer taste because juices and flavours tend to seep in during braising.

Disadvantages of braising

Braising might spoil the texture of the food if carried out for a long time. It also causes the leaching of nutrients in water such as vitamins B and C. Prolonged braising can destroy nutrients such as vitamin C.
