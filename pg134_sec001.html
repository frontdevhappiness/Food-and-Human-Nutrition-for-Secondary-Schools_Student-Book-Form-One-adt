Pulses, nuts and oil seeds

Introduction

Pulses, nuts and oil seeds are foods that contain nutrients such as protein, fat, minerals and vitamins, which are necessary for our bodies. Pulses and nuts also contain beneficial dietary fibres. They are used to make various dishes using different cooking methods. They are found in various shapes, sizes and colours. In this chapter, you will learn about the concepts of pulses, nuts and oil seeds, the nutritive values of pulses and nuts, their uses and the methods of cooking them. The competencies developed will enable you to use pulses, nuts and oil seeds in making different dishes to improve health and earn income.

Concept of Pulses

Pulses are dry edible seeds. They are also known as legumes. Pulses are valued for their nutritional value, affordability and availability. They are low in fat, high in protein, and rich in dietary fibre, making them an excellent source of plant-based protein and other essential nutrients. The commonly used pulses are beans, cowpeas, soya beans, chickpeas, green grams, lima beans, lentils, Bambara nuts and pigeon peas. Like beans, lentils, and chickpeas, pulses have an outer protective layer called a seed coat or skin. Over time, especially in storage conditions with fluctuating temperatures and humidity, the seed coat of pulses can become harder and less permeable, making it difficult to cook.
