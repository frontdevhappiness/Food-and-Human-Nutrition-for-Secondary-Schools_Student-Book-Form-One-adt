Advantages of frying

(i)
The flavour of food is retained and improved.
(ii)
The food acquires a nice appetising brown colour.
(iii)
It shortens cooking time.
(iv)
It gives the food a distinct aroma.

Disadvantages of frying

(i)
High heat treatment can affect water-soluble vitamins, such as vitamin C.
(ii)
Frying adds extra calories to the body due to oil absorption, contributing to weight gain.

Steaming: Steaming is a gentle and healthy cooking method that involves cooking food using steam from boiling water. Vegetables that can be steamed include spinach, broccoli, and other leafy greens such as carrots, cauliflower, green beans and asparagus. Steaming methods include plate steaming, bowl steaming, colander steaming and steaming using food steamer.

General rules for steaming food

The following rules should be observed during steaming:

(i)
The water used should be in a boiling state before you put food in the steamer.
(ii)
The instructions given by the manufacturer should be followed when steaming vegetables using a food steamer.
(iii)
Steam must be produced constantly during cooking.
(iv)
To maintain the water temperature, boiling water should be added when needed.
(v)
The steamer or a pan must have a tight-fitting lid to avoid loss of steam. The colander and plate should fit well on the saucepan.
(vi)
When opening the lid, direct the steam away from you to avoid getting scalds.
