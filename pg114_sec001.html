Nourishing drinks

Nourishing drinks are beverages that are designed to provide essential nutrients and promote overall health and well-being. These drinks are often rich in vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, and other beneficial compounds that support the body’s functions and help maintain optimal health. Nourishing drinks can be made using a variety of natural ingredients, including fruits, vegetables, herbs, nuts, seeds, and dairy or plant-based milk alternatives. Some common examples of nourishing drinks include fruit juice, milk, soup, yoghurt, enriched porridge and smoothies. Nourishing drinks are important for a person who has lost appetite due to an illness. They facilitate the recovery process and hydrate the body.

Recipe for the preparation of an orange banana smoothie and banana milkshake

Orange banana smoothie

Ingredients (serve 1 person)

  • 4 ripe bananas
  • 4 tablespoon yoghurt
  • 2 oranges
  • 250 ml water or milk
  • Ice cubes (optional)

Method

  • 1. Wash, peel and cut bananas into small pieces.
  • 2. Cut and squeeze the oranges to obtain the juice.
  • 3.
    Add the pieces of banana, water or milk, orange juice and yoghurt to the blender. Blend them until smooth.
  • 4. Pour the mixture into a jug and chill or add ice cubes (Optional).
  • 5. Serve it accordingly.
