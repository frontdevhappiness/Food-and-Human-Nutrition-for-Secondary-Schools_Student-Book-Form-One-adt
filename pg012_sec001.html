Relationship between Food and Human Nutrition with other subjects

Food and Human Nutrition, and Mathematics: Food and Human Nutrition applies knowledge and skills from other subjects which relate to it. For example, measuring the amount of ingredients needed to prepare a healthy and nutritious meal involves the application of Mathematics.

Food and Human Nutrition, and Physics: Food and Human Nutrition is also related to Physics in several aspects. A good example is cooking food using a pressure cooker. As water boils, steam is produced, pressure increases and the boiling point is raised. The higher temperature and pressure inside the cooker enable the food to cook very quickly. This is an application of Physics. Similarly, cooking pots, cookers, microwaves and refrigerators are designed to work under the principles of Physics.
