Carrot juice

Ingredients (serve 2 people)

  • 5 large carrots
  • 500 ml water
  • 50 g sugar

Method

  1. 1. Make syrup by dissolving sugar in warm water, and then leave it to cool.
  2. 2.

    Wash the carrots thoroughly with clean and safe water. Blanching process can be done to kill microbes.

  3. 3. Slice them thinly into small pieces.
  4. 4. Put them into a blender and add syrup or sugar and water.
  5. 5.

    Blend them until smooth. Strain the juice and then chill.

  6. 6. Serve the juice accordingly.
