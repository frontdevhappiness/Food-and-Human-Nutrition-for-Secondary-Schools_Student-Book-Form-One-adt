Fruits and vegetables

Introduction

Fruits and vegetables are perishable foods. They contain large amounts of water. Therefore, they should be used immediately after harvesting to avoid spoilage. They also contain essential vitamins, minerals and dietary fibre for promoting health. In this chapter, you will learn about fruits and vegetables, types of fruits, classes of vegetables, and nutritive values of fruits and vegetables. You will also learn about the storage of fruits and vegetables. The competencies developed will enable you to use fruits and vegetables properly in order to improve your family’s health and income.

Concept of fruits

A fruit is a flesh, edible part of a plant. It may or may not contain seeds. It is typically sweet, sour or bitter. Fruits can be eaten raw such as ripe bananas, oranges, mangoes and peaches or cooked to make various dishes. For example, an apple is an ingredient for making apple charlotte, a pineapple is used to make pineapple upside down cake and a ripe banana is used to make banana fritters. They can also be raw materials for making refreshing and nourishing beverages.
