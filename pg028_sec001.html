Retention of nutrients during foods preparation can be done in the following ways:

  • (i)
    When peeling foods like potatoes, carrots and fruits, use a sharp knife to peel them thinly to avoid losing the nutrients found under the peel.
  • (ii)
    Wash fruits and vegetables before chopping to avoid loss of vitamins through leaching into the water. Use a sharp knife when chopping.
  • (iii)
    Avoid exposing vegetables to direct sunlight to prevent the loss of vitamins, such as vitamin C.
  • (iv)
    Fruits and vegetables should be prepared just a few minutes before serving.
  • (v)
    Fruits and vegetables should not be soaked because water-soluble vitamins can leach into the soaking water.

Cooking

When some foods are cooked in water, nutrients are dissolved in them. If the water is discarded, these nutrients are lost. Cooking leafy vegetables for too long can destroy some vitamins.

The retention of nutrients during cooking can be done in the following ways:

  • (i)
    Cook leafy vegetables for a short period, just before serving and avoid overcooking as it will cause loss of some nutrients.
  • (ii)
    Cook food with little water and serve the food with its liquid to avoid losing water-soluble vitamins.
  • (iii)
    Prepare enough food for a moment to avoid leftovers which will need reheating, causing loss of nutrients.
  • (iv)
    It is advisable to cook vegetables for a short time to reduce the loss of heat-sensitive nutrients such as B vitamins and vitamin C.
  • (v)
    Cover the food when cooking as some of the water-soluble vitamins like B-vitamins and vitamin C can evaporate.
