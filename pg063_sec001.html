Urojo

Ingredients (serve 6 people)

  • 5 small Irish potatoes
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric
  • 4 tablespoons wheat flour
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • A pinch of chilli powder (optional)
  • 1.5 litres water
  • 1 raw mango
  • 10 ml lime juice

Method

  1. 1. Put the potatoes in a cooking pot and boil them. Do not overcook them. Remove the potatoes from the water and allow them to cool.
  2. 2. Peel and cut the mango in half.
  3. 3. Cut the half mango into small pieces.
  4. 4. In another cooking pot, add 1 litre of water, turmeric and pieces of mangoes. Then, bring them to a boil.
  5. 5. In a mixing bowl, mix the wheat flour with 150 ml of water until lump-free. Then, add the mixture to the boiling water slowly while stirring. Make sure that the mixture has good consistency.
  6. 6. Simmer for about 5 minutes while stirring.
  7. 7. Add lemon juice, salt and chilli powder. Then, continue simmering while stirring until cooked.
  8. 8. Peel the boiled potatoes and dice into cubes. Then, add them to the mixture; stir and continue simmering for about 3–5 minutes.
  9. 9. Serve with toppings or accompaniment, as shown in Figure 4.9.
