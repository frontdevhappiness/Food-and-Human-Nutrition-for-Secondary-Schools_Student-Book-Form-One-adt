Tamarind juice

Ingredients (serve 4 people)

  • 250 g of tamarind
  • 3 tablespoons sugar or 2 tablespoons honey
  • 1500 ml Water
  • Fresh mint leaves (optional)
  • Ice cubes
  • Honey

Method

  1. 1. Prepare syrup by dissolving sugar in warm water and then leave it to cool.
  2. 2. Wash the tamarind and remove the shell.
  3. 3. Soak the tamarind in a bowl using clean and safe water.
  4. 4. Leave it for one hour.
  5. 5. Squeeze out the juice from the tamarind as much as possible.
  6. 6. Strain the juice into a jug, add syrup or honey and then stir it well.
  7. 7. Chill or add ice cubes.
  8. 8. Add mint leaves, then serve the juice accordingly.
