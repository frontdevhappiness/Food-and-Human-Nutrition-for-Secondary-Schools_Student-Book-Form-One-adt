Mint and ginger lemon drink

Ingredients (serve 2 people)

  • ½ teaspoon grated ginger
  • 3 lemons
  • 200 ml water
  • Ice cubes
  • Mint leaves
  • 200 ml carbonated drink

Method

Ingredients (serve 3 people)

  • 1. Wash the ginger, lemon and mint leaves.
  • 2. Extract the juice from ginger.
  • 3. Squeeze the lemon to obtain juice.
  • 4. Mix all the juices and carbonated drinks.
  • 5. Add ice cubes and mint leaves.
  • 6. Serve it accordingly.
