Stability of food nutrients

Different foods contain different types of nutrients, which can be altered through food preparation, cooking and storage. Therefore, retention of nutrients during these processes is important. This will help in preventing nutrient loss.

Food preparation

During food preparation, food may come in contact with water, heat and air. These elements may affect the stability of food nutrients. There may be noticeable changes in appearance, texture, and flavour. Nutritive values of foods may also be affected when subjected to different food preparation methods. For example, allowing leafy vegetables to remain soaked in washing water or shredding them too thinly can lead to a loss of water-soluble vitamins (vitamin B group and C). Vitamin B₁ can also be lost through washing rice. Soaking rice in water allows certain B vitamins to leach into the water. Exposing fruit juices to air may result in the loss of vitamin C.
