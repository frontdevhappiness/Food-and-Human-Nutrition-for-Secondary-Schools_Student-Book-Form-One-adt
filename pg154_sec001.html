Proper nutrition

Introduction

Proper nutrition is important for the body to stay active and perform its functions effectively. It is a result of consuming a balanced meal, which is required for life. A person who takes proper nutrition can participate in various economic activities and raise his or her standard of living. In this chapter, you will learn about the concept of proper nutrition, the importance of proper nutrition on health and the relationship between diet, health and physical activities. You will also learn about various eating disorders. The competencies developed will enable you to make informed dietary choices and prevent Non-Communicable Diseases(NCDs), and eating disorders, you will also be able to educate the community at large.

The concept of proper nutrition

Proper nutrition means getting a balanced meal together with performing regular physical activities such as running and walking. Good health depends on proper nutrition. Proper nutrition is essential for maintaining and promoting optimal health and well-being throughout life. Making informed dietary choices and adopting healthy eating habits are crucial steps in enhancing overall health and preventing nutrition-related health disorders.

Proper nutrition is also important in achieving healthy lifestyle. It refers to an individual’s way of living, including behaviours and choices that lower the risk of becoming ill or dying early. A healthy lifestyle includes the following aspects:
