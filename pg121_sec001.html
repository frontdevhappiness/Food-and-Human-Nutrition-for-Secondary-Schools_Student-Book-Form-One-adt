Hors d’oeuvres

A hors d’oeuvre consists of a variety of highly flavoured, well-seasoned and colourful foods served in small portions. It can be served while hot or cold and may be served at a cocktail party, where there is no full dinner. The hors d’oeuvre can be served at a table or passed on trays among guests. Examples of hors d’oeuvres are canapés made up of cucumber base, bread, pastries or crackers like tarts. The bases of canapés can be spread with cream cheese, or butter and then topped with fruits, meat or seafood, such as fish. Other examples are skewered meat and fish.

Cucumber canapés

Ingredients (serve 4 person)

  • 1 large cucumber
  • Pinch salt (optional)
  • 1 firm ripe tomato
  • Parsley
  • 100 g herbs
  • 50 g cream cheese

Method

  1. 1.
    Wash and chop the tomato.
  2. 2.
    Wash and trim the end of the cucumber. Peel the cucumber in alternating stripes. Slice the cucumber into half-inch slices.
  3. 3.
    Place herbs and cream cheese in a bowl and stir it until smooth. Then, add it to a piping bag fitted with a tip pipe. Pipe the cheese onto each cucumber slice. Top with chopped tomatoes and parsley. Sprinkle salt if desired.
  4. 4.
    Serve it as desired.
