Nutrient deficiency condition or disorder
Vitamin B3
(Niacin / nicotic acid)
(i) Proper functioning of the nervous and digestive systems
(ii) Promotes growth in children
(iii) Maintains the health of the skin
(iv) Assists in releasing energy from carbohydrates
(i) Animal sources: eggs, liver, fish, meat, yeast, poultry, kidney and milk
(ii) Plant sources: green vegetables, whole cereal grains, whole meal bread and cassava
(i) Pellagra
(ii) Mouth sores
(iii) Loss of appetite
(iv) Depression
(v) Weakness
Vitamin B6
(i) Assists in the metabolism of fats and proteins

(i) Animal sources: meat, liver, fish
(ii) Plant sources: sweet potatoes, white beans, maize, avocados, bananas and cabbage
(i) Anaemia
(ii) Irritability
(iii) Muscle twitching
(iv) Convulsions
(v) Skin lesions
(vi) Mouth sores
Vitamin B9
(Folic acid/folate)
(i) Synthesis of new cells
(ii) Assists in the metabolism of carbohydrates
(i) Animal sources: liver, kidney and fish
(ii) Plant sources: green leafy vegetables, legumes, groundnuts, oil seeds and folate fortified foods
(i) Birth defects
(ii) Anaemia
(iii) Poor hearing
(iv) Frequent infections
(v) Muscle and joint pains
(vi) Bleeding gums
