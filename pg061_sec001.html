Dishes made from fruits

Fruits are used to make different dishes such as a pineapple upside down cake, urojo, and banana fritters. They are also used to make beverages.

Banana fritters

Ingredients (serve 3 people)

  • 250 g wheat flour
  • 2 g salt
  • 4 g baking powder
  • 50 g icing sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 150 ml milk or water
  • 6 ripe bananas
  • 500 ml cooking oil

Method

  1. 1. Make a thick batter from wheat flour, eggs, milk or water, salt and baking powder.
  2. 2. Peel each banana and cut it vertically into two halves.
  3. 3. Coat the cut bananas with batter.
  4. 4. Heat the oil until fairly hot. Add the coated bananas and fry them until golden brown.
  5. 5. Drain them to remove excess oil.
  6. 6. Serve them on a plate and dredge them with icing sugar, as shown in Figure 4.8.
