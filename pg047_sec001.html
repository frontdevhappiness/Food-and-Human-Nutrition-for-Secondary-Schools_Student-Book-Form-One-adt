Table: Sources of accidents and safety precautions in the kitchen

Source of accidents

Safety precaution

Kitchen equipment such as knives and cookers (a) Knives

(i) When using sharp knives always cut in a direction away from you. (ii) Always use a chopping board to minimize the risk of cutting your hand. (iii) Store knives out of children reach and make sure sharp brades are point down.

(b) Cooker

(i) Turn off burners and hot plates after use. (ii) Use oven gloves to remove food items from the oven. (iii) Turn the pan’s handle inward during cooking.

Kitchen activities such as frying and steaming (a) Frying

(i) Do not overfill the pan with the oil during frying. (ii) Heat the oil to its required temperature but not higher to avoid flaming.

(b) Steaming

Never put your face close to the pan when removing the lid.

Clothing and foot wear

(i) Keep sleeves, scarves and ties away from flames. (ii) Do not wear high-heel or loose-fitting shoes to avoid falling.

Electrical instruments such as plugs

(i) Do not touch plugs with wet hands to avoid getting a shock. (iii) Electrical equipment should be serviced regularly.

Movement

(i) Do not run in the kitchen as you may fall. (iv) Never walk around with sharp, hot or heavy items.

Cleaning materials

Store cleaning materials safely and out of reach of children.
