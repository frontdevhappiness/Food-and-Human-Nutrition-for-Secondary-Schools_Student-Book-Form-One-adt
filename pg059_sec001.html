Preparing fruits before consumption

Consider the following points when preparing fruits for consumption.

(i)

Wash the fruits before eating. Germs on the peel or skin can get inside the fruits during cutting. Wash the fruits even if you do not plan to eat their peels. Do not wash fruits with soap, detergent or commercial produce, since it may introduce residues that may affect the consumer’s health.

(ii)

Remove the stalk from the fruit. Then, clean the fruit well using clean safe water. Dry it well to remove any bacteria that may be present.

(iii)

Use a clean sharp knife for peeling. If necessary, peel it thinly.

(iv)

To retain vitamin C content, slice or cut the fruit and serve it immediately.
