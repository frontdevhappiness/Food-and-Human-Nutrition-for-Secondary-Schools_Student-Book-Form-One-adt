Negative effects of beverages

Beverages play significant roles in our bodies; however, unhealthy beverages may have some negative effects. These beverages include artificially sweetened juice, carbonated drinks and alcoholic drinks. These drinks have adverse impacts on body health when consumed in excess. For example, they may cause overweight and obesity and increase the risk of non-communicable diseases, such as cardiovascular diseases and type 2 diabetes. Overconsumption of caffeine and tannin in coffee and tea may also lead to stimulating and dehydrating effects on the body, particularly anxiety and insomnia. Likewise, pregnant women should reduce or avoid consuming caffeinated drinks, because they may increase the risk of miscarriage.
