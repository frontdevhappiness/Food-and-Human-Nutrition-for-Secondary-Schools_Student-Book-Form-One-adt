Preparation of soups

Watch reliable online videos or read from other sources on the preparation of different soups.

(i)
From the sources, select a recipe for one type of the soup.
(ii)
Prepare the soup and assess its quality.

Qualities of a good soup

A good soup should have the following qualities:

(i) A rich flavour depending on the ingredients used: Sufficient seasoning should be used to bring out the flavour.
(ii)

Not greasy. This can be avoided by not using too much oil when preparing the soup

(iii)

A good soup should be balanced and nourishing. The soup should contain a variety of ingredients, including vegetables, lean protein and complex carbohydrates that work together to provide a balanced and nutritious meal.

(iv)

A good soup should be visually appealing. The soup should be served in a beautiful bowl or plate that shows off its colours and textures.

(v)

With the right consistency, ingredients and preparation, a soup may have a thick and right consistency, depending on its type. The soup should not have lumps.

(vi) Finely chopped ingredients that easily fit onto a spoon.
(vii) Well-balanced ingredients with each element contributing to the overall dish without overpowering others.

Note: Do not overcook the soup to retain its flavour. If stock is used in making the soup, it should be fresh and well-flavoured. Soup can be served with foods such as croutons, bread rolls, or slices of bread.
