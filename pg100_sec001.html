Beverages and appetizers

Introduction

Beverages and appetizers nourish the body by providing the necessary nutrients for health. Beverages and appetizers can be either liquid or solid foods. Some fruits and vegetables are used to make beverages and appetizers. In this chapter, you will learn about beverages and appetizers, their types and how to prepare them. You will also learn about the negative effects of beverages. The competencies developed will enable you to prepare various beverages and appetizers for individual and family use or business purposes.

The concept of beverages

Beverages are hot or cold drinks which are specifically prepared for human consumption. They are served as drinks with or without meals. Examples of beverages are tea, coffee, milk, coconut milk, alcoholic drinks and soft drinks such as fruits and carbonated drinks. Some beverages provide nutrients to our bodies and maintain body fluid levels. Selecting beverages is based on age, lifestyle, the weather, health condition, and occasion. The following points show the importance of taking beverages:

(i)

Beverages provide essential fluids for hydrating body organs such as the eyes, nose, mouth and lungs as well as tissues for them to function properly. However, some beverages such as alcohol and concentrated coffee have diuretic effects; they remove fluids from the body and cause dehydration.

(ii)

They replace the water lost from the body through sweating, excretion and breathing, hence, they help maintaining the level of body fluids.

(iii)

Beverages help us to quench our thirst.
